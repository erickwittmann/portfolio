20 Days of Motion

A small experiment: one looping animation a day, for twenty days.

Soundtrack: : armani white - goated. (ft. denzel curry)

I made these in Professor Austin Shaw’s motion graphics class at RIT. The brief: make a new loop every day and post it. Three things stuck with me:

  1. Looping. Animations that end where they start, so they can play forever without a seam.
  2. Posting consistently. Twenty days in a row on Instagram, each loop its own reel with its own track.
  3. Matching sound to picture. The edit strings all twenty together and cuts them to the music.
