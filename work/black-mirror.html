Show / Story Background

Black Mirror is a critically acclaimed anthology series created by Charlie Brooker that explores the dark, thought-provoking intersections between technology, society, and human behavior.

Each standalone episode presents a speculative near-future scenario, serving as both cautionary tale and cultural commentary. The show’s evolving timeline mirrors real world technological shifts moving from early social media anxieties to AI, surveillance, and digital identity.

02
